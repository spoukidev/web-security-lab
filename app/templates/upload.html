Root cause

Filename and request MIME fields are attacker-controlled metadata. They do not prove a file's actual type.

Secure checks

Check the allowed extension, declared MIME, bytes/signature, size, and generate the destination name. Keep storage outside executable or public paths.

Security lesson

Validation reduces risk but uploads still need least-privilege permissions, isolation, monitoring, and careful download handling.
