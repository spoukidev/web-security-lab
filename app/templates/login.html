Authentication

Authentication establishes an identity from a valid token. Weak secrets or skipped validation make that identity untrustworthy.

Authorization

A valid token is not permission to access every resource. Every action still needs server-side authorization, as the IDOR lab demonstrates.

Secure validation

Pin the accepted algorithm, verify the signature and time-based claims, validate issuer/audience where applicable, and keep signing keys secret and rotatable.
